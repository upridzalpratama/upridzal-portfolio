Want to Sayang

Video Content Strategy2024
29K+Peak TikTok views
Objective

Grow awareness for a blind date event organizer and relationship consultancy, and keep event batches filled.

Challenge
  • Static romance-quote posts were consistently underperforming.
  • Content needed to feel relatable, not like an advertisement.
Approach
  1. Diagnosed why the quote posts were not getting reach.
  2. Pivoted to a casual, conversational video format with 2 to 3 people.
  3. Built topics around relatable romance situations.
  4. Distributed the videos across Instagram and TikTok.
Result
  • Consistent 1,000+ views per Instagram video.
  • Peak of 29,000+ views on TikTok, 54K views in 90 days.
  • 50K+ reach and 30+ participants per event batch.
